Step 1 — Foundations

What Is a Segmentation?

Think of Marketo Segmentations like a filing cabinet. Every person in your database gets sorted into exactly one drawer — based on rules you define.

The Filing Cabinet Analogy

Your database has millions of contacts. A segmentation is a set of labelled drawers. Marketo automatically assigns every person to one drawer, and re-sorts them dynamically as their data changes.

You can have many filing cabinets (segmentations), each organised by a different dimension — job level, geography, product interest, and so on.

Step 2 — Why We're Doing This

Two Clear Goals

This project is driven by two core objectives for the marketing team.

Goal #1

Standardise Target Audiences

Make it easier and faster for marketers to choose and build email audiences — removing guesswork and inconsistency.

  • Easier audience selection for campaign requesters
  • Faster build time for email senders
  • Visibility into audience sizing before sending
  • Target only the most engaged audiences
Goal #2

Protect the CXO "White Glove" List

Identify and ring-fence a premium group of C-Level / F500 / G2K contacts so they are only emailed by the CXO team — never by regular marketing campaigns.

  • Separate segment: CXO — Do Not Use
  • Automatically excluded from all mass sends
  • Only the CXO transformation team may contact them

Step 8 — Compliance & Marketability

Compliance Group Flow

Every person is sorted into a compliance group based on their country. This determines the opt-in rules that apply before you can email them.

🔴

Explicit — Double

Must confirm opt-in TWICE

Germany · Austria · Switzerland
🟡

Explicit — Single

Must opt-in once before contact

Most of Europe · Australia · Japan · Brazil · Canada · many more
🟢

Implied

Can contact unless they opt out

United States · India · many global markets
⛔

Restricted

Do not contact. Sanctioned or restricted countries

Russia · Iran · North Korea · many others
❓

Unknown

No policy — usually no country populated in the record

No country value in system

Step 9 — White Glove Rule

CXO Protection

The most important rule in the entire segmentation project.

⭐

CXO Audience — Do Not Use (DNU)

A premium group of C-Level executives at Fortune 500 / Global 2000 companies. These contacts are ring-fenced and must never appear in a regular marketing email batch.

✅ Who qualifies: C-Level job level / title + F500 or G2K account tier
🚫 Excluded from ALL regular marketing sends automatically
👔 ONLY the CXO Transformation Team may email them
⚠️ Daniela must validate the rules before segment is activated
